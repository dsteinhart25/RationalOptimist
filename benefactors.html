Our Benefactors

The Rational Optimist Society is a not-for-profit endeavor. We are grateful for the financial support of our benefactors, who share our belief that rational optimism is worth championing.

Mauldin Economics

Founded by John Mauldin — a founding member of the Rational Optimist Society — Mauldin Economics is one of the most widely read independent economic research firms in the world. John's weekly letter, Thoughts from the Frontline, was one of the first free e-letters to deliver independent research to individual investors.

Mauldin Economics provides financial support to the Rational Optimist Society, helping fund our mission to connect rational optimists worldwide.

RiskHedge

RiskHedge is an independent investment research firm focused on disruptive innovation and transformative technologies. As part of the Rational Optimist Society's alliance network, RiskHedge shares our conviction that breakthrough technologies will continue to reshape the world for the better.

RiskHedge provides financial support to the Rational Optimist Society, helping us celebrate and connect with the innovators pushing boundaries.
